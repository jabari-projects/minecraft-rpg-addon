# Classes

Players choose one primary class and can layer it with a combat subclass and public skills. Class, subclass, XP, skill, resource, morph, and cooldown state are persisted through the add-on's scoreboard-backed systems.

## Vampire

Vampires use **plasma** as their class resource and have a hunger-lock passive. Their progression centers on feeding with Vampire Fangs, Blood Thirst life steal and plasma recovery, Midnight Strength, Bat Morph, and Vampire Troop summons. Compel is the plasma-costing ultimate, while Sun Immunity is the final toggleable skill; the custom Vampire armor set also provides sunlight protection and plasma-drain support.

## Werewolf

Werewolves focus on aggressive melee pressure and mobility. Their kit includes poison-capable fangs, Roar wolf allies, Shadow Wolf Morph, repeatable Lycan Vitality, and the Scent ultimate that suppresses hostile-mob aggression until provoked. The Shadow Wolf form supplies the class's strongest durability and movement package.

## Banshee

Banshees are a control and mobility class with Sonic Scream, persistent invisibility, constrained wall phasing, and a spectral morph. Mind Fracture adds nausea to Banshee attacks, and the Soul ultimate heals the player to full health after Sonic Scream kills. The class trades conventional durability for positional tools and health-cost-based offense.

## Human

Humans are the primary-class baseline. They do not receive a separate primary active-ability set; instead, they use public skills and the selected combat subclass to create a conventional survival build.

## Combat subclasses

Every primary class can be paired with one of five combat roles: **Warrior** (returning weapon and charge), **Ninja** (mobility, projectiles, and stealth), **Witch** (spells, necromancy, and staff specialization), **Archer** (bow modifications), or **Tank** (crowd control and mitigation).
